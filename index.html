Drag with one finger to pan. Pinch with two fingers to zoom. Tap a tab to compare. Physical-device validation is still in progress.

Drag to pan · Wheel to zoom

Pan and Region are below the image tabs. Side by side chooses two to four images; Difference chooses two sources and a fixed calculation plane. The visible Range control shows the committed display endpoints shared by all tabs. Full range uses the selected source’s declared range, never observed brightness or camera white-level metadata.

On phones and compact windows, Position opens the shared X/Y controls. Go applies the coordinates; Close or Escape keeps an unsubmitted draft. Zoom and pan padlocks remain beside their controls. Range and Position sheets do not resize the image. Use Zoom presets or percentage entry as well as gestures.

Arrows: 1 pixel · Shift + arrows: 10 pixels

Desktop Region tool: click two corners to measure a region. Hold Space and drag to pan. Arrows and Enter select pixels; Escape cancels an unfinished region. Rename regions and expand/collapse their statistics in the Regions inspector. Pan keeps your regions. Use Remove all ROIs to clear them. Variance divides by N (population variance); variance is in squared original-sample units.

Option/Alt + ←/→: switch tabs

Option/Alt + 1–9: choose tab

+ / −: zoom · F: fit

L: lights out. Black out the interface while keeping the image and its tabs visible. Press L again to restore normal brightness. Available outside text fields, menus and dialogs.

The padlocks beside Zoom and X/Y independently lock zoom and pan across all image tabs. Unlock to use those controls again. Fit needs both unlocked. Image bounds still apply after zooming, resizing or changing image dimensions.

View selects RGB, R, G, B or Luma across all tabs and the navigator. Individual planes are grayscale and ignore alpha. Luma uses original samples without gamma correction.

Compare shows two, three or four images in a grid. Drag, wheel or pinch in any cell to move them together. Select a cell’s name to inspect that image. Choose images changes the grid; closing Compare keeps the image tabs. Closing a source removes it from the grid; fewer than two closes Compare. Different sizes share source coordinates, with blank areas beyond smaller images. Fit shows the combined image extent. The padlocks still prevent navigation. Create ROIs in an image tab; existing rectangles and the selected image’s statistics remain available in Compare.

Diff compares two matching original images. Choose RGB or a single comparison plane; Luma calculates |Luma(A) − Luma(B)| with fractional precision. A result’s calculation stays fixed when its View changes. A luma view of an RGB result weights the RGB differences. Lower the display-range maximum to reveal small differences. Closing source tabs keeps the result available.

Bayer RAW: File → Custom RAW opens sensor dumps using explicit header bytes, dimensions, depth and packing. Advanced settings handle stride, word alignment, individual header offsets and session layout profiles. Preview and Cancel leave the viewer unchanged. Camera DNG, ARW, CR2/CR3 and NEF use Open images; compatibility depends on the camera and compression. No embedded preview substitutes for raw data.

Mosaic / R / Gr / Gb / B preserve the sensor grid. Gr is green in a red row; Gb is green in a blue row. Neutral masked sites are not zero-valued measurements. Use 100% to avoid sparse-plane aliasing. Raw differences require matching declared precision, CFA phase, coordinates and decoded domain; camera files with unknown precision are inspectable but cannot be compared.

Range mapping preserves original samples. Embedded color profiles and gamma corrections are not applied.
